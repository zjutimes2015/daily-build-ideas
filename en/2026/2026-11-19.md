## Today's Build Idea: PatchCheck — "Is My Server Exposed?"

**Product:** One-click nginx RCE exposure check + CVE monitoring for small teams.

**For whom:** Small self-hosting teams, indie hackers running their business on a VPS, and freelance ops people / agencies serving small clients — no security team, and every CVE means figuring it out alone.

**Core features:**

1. Paste a domain, get a verdict in 30 seconds: is HTTP/2 on, is the version in the affected range, is it already patched to 1.30.7 / 1.31.9.
2. A plain-English fix: which version to upgrade to, or disable HTTP/2 as a stopgap, with copy-paste commands.
3. Honest uncertainty: when distro packaging lags and the version string can't tell you, say "can't tell" and link the distro security tracker instead of guessing.
4. Monitoring: register 10 domains, get an email / Telegram alert within an hour when the next CVE hits your stack.

**Monetization:** Single check free; monitoring $9/mo (10 domains); one-time full-site report for agencies, $19.

**Why this is a small-team opportunity:** Snyk and Dependabot are built for enterprises and flood you with alerts. Small teams don't need "another vulnerability database" — they need one sentence: "you're exposed / you're fine + here's what to do now." That verdict is the product.

**Safety line:** Non-invasive checks only (read version headers, probe the HTTP/2 switch). No exploit traffic. Only check your own domains.

## Why Now

- **On 2026-11-18, Wiz and four other research groups jointly disclosed two nginx HTTP/2 unauthenticated RCEs**, both Critical at CVSS 4.0 9.2. CVE-2026-123007 affects 1.9.5–1.31.8 with HTTP/2 on; CVE-2026-123633 affects 1.19.7–1.31.8.
- **Fixes are out, but distro packages lag.** 1.30.7 (stable) / 1.31.9 (mainline) shipped on Nov 18, yet as of Nov 19 Debian had no fixed package across releases — the next few days are the "am I exposed?" window.
- **Attention is at its peak.** #1 on Hacker News today: 1,275 points, 552 comments; the same story is on Lobsters' hottest list. Anxiety and search are maxed out.
- **A clean window.** Wiz is not releasing exploits, and as of Nov 19 there are no reports of in-the-wild exploitation — building on the defensive side carries no ethical baggage.
- **Every major CVE is an acquisition event.** People who come for nginx today will come back for the next Heartbleed-level bug; monitoring is the retention.
