## Today's Build Idea: Neon Review — "The New #1 Coding Model, Reviewing Your PRs"

**Product:** An automated GitHub PR review bot powered by Gemini 4 Neon.

**For whom:** Indie hackers and small teams who can't afford a dedicated reviewer — and whose Copilot plans don't include Neon (it's gated to Pro+ / Max / Business / Enterprise); plus open-source maintainers drowning in PRs.

**Core features:**

1. Install in 2 minutes: a GitHub App that reviews new PRs automatically, verdict in under 2 minutes.
2. Verdict first: "ship it / fix these 3 things" up top — not a wall of nits.
3. Cost-transparent: every review shows the tokens it spent, so users can do their own math.
4. Learns your repo: picks up conventions from merged PRs (naming, test requirements, banned patterns) instead of applying generic rules to everyone.

**Monetization:** Free for 20 PRs/mo; $12/mo unlimited repos (solo); $29/mo team (5 seats). Cost math (estimated from Google's official $3 / $15 per 1M tokens): a typical PR review at ~50k input + 5k output tokens costs about $0.23; a $12 plan breaks even at roughly 50 PRs/mo, with light users subsidizing heavy ones.

**Why this is a small-team opportunity:** Copilot already has Neon, but behind a paywall tier; enterprise review tools sell "compliance + full DevOps", while small teams just want one sentence: "can this PR merge, and what will break?" The model is new — the verdict is the product.

**Honest limits:** The model isn't exclusive — anyone can call the Gemini API, so the moat is verdict UX + repo-convention learning, not the model itself; a third-party index score doesn't equal results on your own repo, so run a side-by-side evaluation on your own PRs in week one.

## Why Now

- **On 2026-11-18, Google made Gemini 4 Neon generally available.** Its launch post positions it for "complex software engineering, knowledge work, and science workflows", priced at $3 / $15 per 1M tokens (input / output), with a 1M-token context window.
- **On 2026-11-19–20, Neon took #1 on the Models Engineering Index (68.4)**, ahead of OpenAI's 6.2 Astra (67.1) and Anthropic's Fable 5.2 (66.7); #1 on Hacker News today with 359 points and 235 comments.
- **GitHub Copilot shipped Neon the same day — an authenticity check from the incumbent** — but only for Pro+ / Max / Business / Enterprise, leaving a gap for a cheap standalone.
- **Every change of the guard is a 2–4 week window.** Every dev tool that hard-coded Claude / GPT as "the best model" has to re-tune; a step late means copying someone else's parameters.
- **A 1M context window makes whole-repo review cheap enough to sell at $12/mo for the first time.** Review bots that used to stitch files together couldn't afford this price with this quality.
